# Whiteboard interface
A compact analyst's whiteboard beside the battle. Operate mode: users scan known facts, inspect incoming damage, and compare possible sets.

White (#fff) surface, charcoal (#202522) ink, gray (#59615d) secondary text, fine gray (#d8ddd9) dividers, and marker blue (#245dc1) for observed facts and focus. No gradients, decorative textures, external fonts, or Pokémon art. Abstract open-circle mark and line-based HP/damage measurements are the identity.

Use system sans-serif at 12px, 1.5 line height; headings 18px. Numeric damage uses tabular figures. Sidebar width 264px, padding 12px; body capped at 60vh / 580px, sections separated by 16px and rules. Sets are open text groups, never nested cards. Buttons and summaries have visible keyboard focus. Narrow viewports collapse into a bottom-positioned, height-limited board. Respect reduced motion.

Keep estimates qualified. Unknown, removed, observed and possible are different states. Show calculation failure separately from zero damage.
