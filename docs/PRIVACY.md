# Privacy policy — PS Randbats Assistant

Effective September 15, 2026.

PS Randbats Assistant processes Gen 9 Random Battle messages on play.pokemonshowdown.com to display damage estimates and possible opposing sets. This includes the player's own team information supplied by Showdown and publicly revealed opponent information.

## Information processed locally

- **Website content:** battle information, including Pokémon, moves, HP, items, abilities, stat boosts, and team information supplied to the player by Showdown.
- **User activity:** incoming Showdown battle messages are monitored to keep calculations and set tracking current. Interface interactions such as hovering over a move control its damage tooltip; they are not logged.
- **Web history:** the current Showdown page path, URL fragment, and battle room identifiers are read to associate the sidebar with the correct battle. The extension does not access your browser's general browsing history or other websites.
- **Personally identifiable information:** battle message payloads may contain Showdown player usernames. These may be processed incidentally with battle messages but are not used for profiling or retained as a separate list.

## Purpose, storage, and sharing

Processing happens locally in your browser solely to provide damage estimates and possible-set tracking. Battle state and room identifiers are held temporarily in memory. The extension does not transmit this information to its developer or third parties, persistently store battle or browsing histories, use analytics, show advertisements, or sell data. No extension account is required.

PS Randbats Assistant's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is not used or transferred for unrelated purposes, advertising, determining creditworthiness, or lending.

The extension uses bundled code and Pokémon set data; it does not download executable code. Pokémon Showdown itself is a separate service with its own privacy practices.

## Your controls and contact

Closing or reloading the page clears in-memory state. After disabling or uninstalling the extension, reload any open Showdown pages to remove code already running in those pages.

For privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on the Chrome Web Store listing or [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/bagel786/ps-randbats-ext/issues). GitHub issues are public; do not include sensitive information. Information you voluntarily send through email or GitHub is handled by those separate services and is outside the extension's local battle processing.
